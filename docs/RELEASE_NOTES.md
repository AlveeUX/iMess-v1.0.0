# Release Notes

Updates shown to everyone in the app, newest first. Add new entries directly below this line.

## August 11, 2026

- **New look.** MessPilot has a fresh orange-and-charcoal color theme and a new app icon.
- **Simpler sign-in.** No more choosing "Admin" or "Member" before signing in — just sign in, and the app shows you the right things automatically based on your account.
- **Redesigned dashboard.** A new welcome banner shows your day's status, the live meal rate, and quick actions at a glance.
- **Meal edits now get a quick check.** Logging a brand-new day is still instant. Editing a day you've already logged now goes to your admin for a quick approval, so the numbers everyone sees stay trustworthy.
- **Bazar safety net.** If a bazar submission gets rejected 3 times in a row, new submissions pause until an admin unblocks you — one approval resets the count back to zero. Rejected entries now have a one-tap **Resubmit** to fix and resend them.
- **Release notes.** You're reading them! Open this any time from the bottom of the sidebar.
