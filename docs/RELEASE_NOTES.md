# Release Notes

Updates shown to everyone in the app, newest first. Add new entries directly below this line.

## August 12, 2026

- **Quick meal approvals.** Admins now see pending meal entries right on the Dashboard, with one-tap Approve/Reject.
- **Fixed: meal approvals were silently failing.** Approving, rejecting, or editing a meal entry could fail behind the scenes since yesterday's update — fixed.
- **See your password.** A show/hide icon on the password field lets you check it before signing in.

## August 11, 2026

- **New look.** Fresh orange-and-charcoal theme and a new app icon.
- **Simpler sign-in.** One sign-in for everyone — no more picking Admin or Member first.
- **Redesigned dashboard.** A welcome banner shows today's status, live meal rate, and quick actions.
- **Meal entries need admin approval.** New or edited meal counts go in as pending until your admin approves them.
- **Smaller meal calendar.** Day cells and badges are more compact, so a full month fits on screen.
- **Future days locked.** Members can't log ahead; admins still can, on a member's behalf.
- **Bazar safety net.** 3 rejected submissions in a row pauses new ones until an admin unblocks you.
- **Inline resubmit.** Fix a rejected bazar entry or deposit right where it sits — no dialog.
- **Release notes.** You're reading them — open anytime from the sidebar.
- **Fixed: Members page.** The member list now loads correctly for everyone.
- **Fixed: Transparency log.** Visible to all members again, and clearly shows who a change was for.
